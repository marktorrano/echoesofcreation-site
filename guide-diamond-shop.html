What Is It?

A premium shop where you can spend Lumina on powerful boost potions, mega consumables, and convenience items. Access it from the Lumina Shop button (top-right HUD) or the side menu (ESC).

Boost Potions (30 min)

EXP Boost: +50% EXP gained (5 Lumina)

Gold Boost: +50% gold drops (5 Lumina)

Drop Rate: +30% item drop rates (8 Lumina)

Damage Boost: +25% all damage (10 Lumina)

Premium Consumables

Mega HP Potion: Restore 1000 HP (3 Lumina)

Mega MP Potion: Restore 500 MP (3 Lumina)

Convenience Items

Portal Scroll Bundle: 10 Village Portal Scrolls (2 Lumina)

Scroll of Protection: Prevents gear destruction on +8 and higher upgrades, stacks to 5 (10 Lumina)

Scroll of Reallocation: Refunds all allocated stat points so you can respec (20 Lumina)

Respec Scroll: Refunds all spent talent points — used from the Talent Tree's Respec button (250 Lumina)

Bag Expansion: Permanently adds 4 inventory slots (15 Lumina)

Storage Expansion: Permanently adds 4 Storage (bank) slots, account-wide, up to 450 total (25 Lumina)

Gender Change Scroll: Switches your character's appearance between male and female (100 Lumina)

Gender Change Scroll

Buy it from the Convenience tab (100 Lumina), then use it from your inventory to switch your character's appearance between male and female. You'll be asked to confirm first.

It's purely cosmetic — your class, level, stats, gear, skills, and progress are all unchanged; only the body model swaps. Other players see your new look too. The scroll is consumed on use; buy another to switch back.
