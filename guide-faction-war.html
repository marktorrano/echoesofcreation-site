An Echoes of Creation guide

Choosing a Faction

Open the Faction panel from the Menu (☰ → Factions) and pick one of three sides — Light (Eloria), Dark (Erevos), or Creation (the Faithful). Your choice binds your WHOLE account, not just one character.

Requires a Lv 30+ character to join.

Tap a faction card to read its lore before you commit.

Switching & Leaving

You can switch sides later, but it costs 500 Lumina and locks your allegiance for 7 days afterward. Leaving a faction forfeits your standing.

You can only belong to one faction at a time.

Joining the War

When a war session is live, a ⚔ Join War button appears in the Faction panel for faction members. Press it to enter the battlefield, where the three factions fight in their own isolated instance.

The war is a base race: each faction has one tower, and the first tower destroyed ends the war — the faction that lands the final blow wins. Close in on an enemy faction's tower and attack it with basic attacks AND your skills; the tower's HP shows in the target bar.

If the button isn't there, the war isn't open right now — check back when a session starts.
