Update v0.2.12

Nothing Left to Guess

Is this an upgrade? Where does that drop? What happened while I was away?

Released August 31, 2026

Eloria has always known more than it told you. Whether the sword you just picked up beats the one on your back. Which monster carries the material you have been farming blind for. What your character got up to for the hour you were away from the keyboard. This update hands all of it over — and where the game was quietly telling you something wrong, it now tells you the truth.

Know What You're Wearing

Open anything in your bag and the answer is right there.

⚖

Side by Side

  • The piece you have equipped opens beside it, full card against full card — upgrade level, sockets, gems and enchants all shown
  • A plain verdict underneath. Attack +12 in green, Defense −3 in red, so you can decide without doing the arithmetic
  • Socketed gems count. Trading away a triple-Sapphire weapon says Skill Damage −9% instead of quietly costing you it
  • Rings and earrings compare against the slot they would actually fill, not a guess
📜

Honest Numbers

  • Magic skills say M.ATK. Every INT skill dealt magic damage already — the tooltip claimed it scaled off physical attack
  • A new Magic tag, and staff and bow skills now mark themselves Ranged. Arcane Bolt and Holy Light always were, and never said so
  • Gear your class can never hold says exactly that, instead of showing tempting numbers for a swap that cannot happen

Find What You Need

The Monsterpedia stopped being a trophy case and became a map.

🔍

Search It

  • Search by monster, by zone, or by item. Type a material and every monster that drops it comes back
  • Monsters you have not met stay hidden, so searching never spoils what is still out there
📍

Location

  • Open a material in your bag and press Location. The Monsterpedia opens on the monsters that drop it, lowest level first
  • The same jump from your Collection — click a material's icon on any row and it takes you there
🎯

Track a Target

  • Press Track on any monster to pin it to your quest tracker
  • It counts kills toward the next reveal, then switches to that monster's collection materials once everything is unlocked

While You Were Away

Power Saving now files a report.

🕔

The Idle Summary

  • Where you are, and for how long — map, level range and channel, beside your idle clock
  • What you earned: EXP, gold, Lumina, and every item that dropped — materials and gear included, which the tally used to miss entirely
  • What it cost you: deaths, and what landed the last hit
⚠

Before It Goes Wrong

  • Potions running low warns you once, in chat, where you can read it over the dimmed screen
  • A bag near full warns too. Once it fills, drops are left on the ground — which has always looked like broken drop rates
  • Both have switches in chat settings, so you can turn either off
💡

Something to Read

  • Tips cycle along the bottom while you are away, fading between them
  • Fourteen new ones — and the tip about right-clicking for auto-cast was wrong. It now tells you to drag a buff potion's slot down

For Crest Holders

One perk that only matters when you are not at the keyboard.

👑

Auto-Revive on Spot

  • Die while auto-hunting and you get back up where you fell, sixty seconds later, and the hunt carries on
  • It spends one of your ten daily on-the-spot revives. When they run out, the normal death screen comes back
  • Off until you switch it on, and it never fires if you respawn yourself first
  • The idle screen tells you where you stand — on with charges left, on with none left, or off

The Marketplace

Price it right, and see what you are buying.

💰

Selling

  • See the competition before you price. The cheapest live asks are listed, not just the lowest — a wall of five at 2,000 is a different market from one stale listing
  • Undercut puts you one Lumina below the cheapest ask, in a single press
🛒

Buying

  • Inspect a listing and it compares against your own gear, the same green and red as your bag, before you spend anything
  • A red dot on the Market icon when Lumina from a sale, or an item you bought, is waiting to be claimed

Comfort

Small things, all day long.

🎨

HUD Themes

  • Light, Dark or Creation — the standard gold, void purple, or creation green
  • Options ▸ Video, applied instantly across your icons, menu and hotbar
📧

Fewer Clicks

  • Claim All in your mail, for the days a compensation wave lands
  • A minimum rarity for the loot feed. Set it to Rare and above and stop scrolling past junk mid-farm
  • Red dots that mean something: a collection ready to register, a daily reward unclaimed, marketplace goods waiting
  • Bag weight shows a percentage beside the numbers, everywhere it appears

Fixed

Things that were wrong, and are not any more.

💬

Chat

  • Your faction tab stops flagging unread on every login for messages you read days ago. Guild, Map and Global did the same thing
⚔

In the Fight

  • Coast monsters heal the way they were designed to again — the Sirens, Revenants and Stalkers of the Dark Tide
  • The white flash on a hit is much fainter. It is there to make a hit land, not to be looked at
🧩

Interface

  • The equipped card shows its real socket count, not the piece's base roll
  • Both compare cards line up, and the tracker stops calling itself Quests when all it holds is a farm target
  • HP and MP potion rings follow your chosen theme like everything else
