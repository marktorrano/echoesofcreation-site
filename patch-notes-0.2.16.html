Update v0.2.16

Solid Ground

Life skills that work on every character, a way out of the floor — and the fixes you asked for by name.

Released September 2026

A shorter update than the last one, and most of it is fixes you asked for by name. Alts can mine again, the guild tower that promised life-skill XP finally pays it, ore counts stop lying, and if the world swallows you there is now a button for that. This is a required update — you will be asked to restart before you can log in.

A Way Out of the Floor

One button, in Options, for the moment the world swallows you.

🧭

Unstuck

  • Options now has an Unstuck button. Fallen through the floor or wedged into a rock? It sets you back on the ground you are standing over, in the zone you are already in
  • Five-minute cooldown. Until now the only way out was a relog
  • It is not a trip home — the walk back is still yours

Life Skills That Work on Every Character

The reports that came in by name, closed by name.

⛏

Alts Can Mine Again

  • An alt on the same account could not mine, forage or fish — it swung the pick, spent nothing and got nothing, because it was reading the main character’s skill levels. Each character now uses its own
  • A lost race at login could gate a main at Mining 1 (“You need Mining 24” at a character who is 24) until the Life Skills panel was opened. The skill fetch now retries
🏗

Wrights’ Hall Pays

  • Wrights’ Hall now actually raises life-skill XP and gather yield by the percent it shows. It has been live since September 6 and paid nothing
⚖

Ore Counts Stop Lying

  • Refining showed Copper Ore 219/5 and refused every click. Ore spent while the overview was still loading was being handed back to the bag; the bag and your account ledger agree again
  • Fresh drops and freshly mined ore vanished seconds after arriving (“chat shows the fragment drop but no fragment”). A policing check was trimming bags to your last cloud save. Fixed server-side on September 18
💎

A Real Patch in Creator’s Gate

  • 16 nodes in one circle, 40 units from the waypoint: 10 voidglass veins (Mining 28) and 6 gloomcap (Foraging 15), instead of five veins scattered across the whole hall
  • Gather nodes no longer spawn inside the Dark Woods fishing pond, and auto-harvest gives up on a vein it cannot reach instead of running at it forever

Combat and Zones

Two server fixes you will feel, and one you will see.

⚔

Monster Cadence

  • Monsters in hosted zones were attacking two to three times faster than intended since the morning of September 19 — Nightmotes and Sunmotes most of all. Cadence is back to normal
💀

Corrupted Zones Stay Up

  • Corrupted zones no longer drop everyone every few hours. A mob respawning after the zone had already emptied was crashing the server
🥂

Slimes Slam

  • Slimes and jellies now wind up and body-slam when they attack instead of bumping into you

Fixes

Smaller things, in the places you spend your time.

🏆

Leaderboard

  • The Level leaderboard ranks same-level players by experience again instead of by Combat Power
✨

Eloria’s Favor

  • Favor is read from the save you are playing. Logging in on another PC no longer shows a stale total from that machine
💰

Lumina Shop

  • Outfits and wings are one-per-account and no longer show a quantity control; prices, controls and Buy line up within each row
  • Wings and outfits bought there are now properly registered as gear, so they can be listed and are never mistaken for an unearned item
📍

Interface

  • The map ping marker is royal blue, so it never reads as quest gold
  • The side-menu footer no longer clips the game name and version
