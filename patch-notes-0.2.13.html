Update v0.2.13

Worth Belonging To

Your guild stops being a chat channel and starts being a reason to log in.

Released September 2026

A guild has been a name over your head and a place to talk. From this update it is something you build. Every member has daily orders to run, the contribution those orders earn buys permanent buffs the whole guild wears, and your own standing inside the guild decides how much of that power actually reaches you. Alongside it, Eloria gets a proper events calendar — one that rotates every week, on its own, without waiting for a patch.

Standing Orders

Five things worth doing, every day, for a guild that keeps what you earn.

📜

The Orders

  • Five standing orders, taken one at a time and repeatable. Slay 100 monsters, earn 100,000 gold, forge or enchant three times, donate 20 materials, or bring down a world boss
  • Each one is worth 20 contribution — to your own standing and to the guild's pooled total, together
  • They sit in your quest tracker like anything else you are working on, with a banner when one completes
  • Progress lands the moment you earn it, not on the next refresh
🛡

Guild Buffs

  • Twelve buff tracks — attack, defense, dodge and accuracy, each in a general, a PvE and a PvP flavour
  • Five levels apiece, bought with the guild's pooled contribution by the leader. They are permanent: once bought, the guild keeps them
  • They do not count toward Combat Power, so a guild buff can never be mistaken for gear you do not have
🎖

Your Grade

  • A personal standing from 1 to 10, earned by running orders, that caps how much of the guild's buff actually reaches you
  • A big guild cannot simply carry you — the buff is bought collectively, but claimed individually
  • Every member's Grade shows on the roster, along with what a low one is costing them

Where You Stand

For the guilds who want it known.

🏆

Guild Rankings

  • A Guilds tab on the leaderboard, ranked by Guild Level, beside the player boards
  • Guild Level rises with lifetime contribution and never falls, so it is a record of everything the guild has ever done rather than what it is holding this week
🎁

Donating

  • The donation screen reads your whole account, not one character — materials in storage count
  • It shows you exactly what is donatable and explains anything greyed out, instead of refusing you at the last step

An Events Calendar

Eloria now runs a schedule, and it keeps itself.

📅

A New Event Every Week

  • Four events rotate, one per week, and then begin again. EXP Rush Hour, Guild Contribution ×2, Collector's Week and Forge Week
  • Three of them run in three-hour windows, three times a day — shown in your own timezone, with a countdown to the next one
  • Guild Contribution week runs around the clock, because a daily order board should never depend on the hour you play
📡

The Events Panel

  • The panel now reads a live list instead of one baked into the game, so a new event can appear without waiting for an update
  • Patch notes have their own section in the panel, opening the release page in your browser
  • And the same list is on the website at echoesofcreation.net/events, so you can check what is running before you log in

Fixed

Things that were wrong, and are not any more.

💰

Economy

  • A gear listing reads your item as it is now, not a stale copy — the bug behind a listed piece losing its upgrades or its sockets
  • Forging a Creation Gem counts Genesis Stones across your whole account. Thirteen players were being told they were short of stones they were visibly carrying
🧩

Interface

  • The guild panel throughout — readable label sizes, consistent buttons and row heights, a working Close, and the buff ladder in its own window rather than shoving the card open
  • Full translations across every supported language, including all of the new guild wording
