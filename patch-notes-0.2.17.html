Update v0.2.17

Five Into One

Reforging — turn the duplicates gathering dust in your warehouse into one piece worth keeping.

Released September 2026

One new system this time, and it is for everyone who has ever looked at five copies of the same boots and sighed. Reforging fuses them into a single fresh piece with a chance of 4–7 sockets, and it always succeeds. This is a required update — restart Steam if it has not downloaded yet.

Reforging

Five copies in, one fresh piece out.

⚒

How It Works

  • Open Reforge from the Menu — or drag it onto your HUD bar with Customize HUD
  • Fuse 5 copies of the same Legend or Genesis piece — a weapon, off-hand, armor piece or cape — into 1 fresh copy
  • The new piece has a chance of 4–7 sockets, and a Reforge never fails
  • Accessories, wings and outfits never hold sockets, so they cannot be reforged
🔍

Choose Your Pieces

  • Pick exactly which copies go in, straight from a view that looks just like your bag — upgrade level, sockets and gems at a glance
  • Auto-add fills the empty slots with your least-invested copies
  • Filters: Ready to reforge (only pieces you hold enough copies of), Weapons, Off-Hand and Armor
⚠

Know What You Give Up

  • Everything on the copies you use is lost — upgrade levels, socketed gems, enchants and Genesis innates. The new piece starts at +0 with empty sockets
  • Before you confirm, Reforging lists exactly what it will destroy, piece by piece
  • Locked pieces are never used — lock anything you want to keep safe
✨

Genesis and Bound Gear

  • Reforged Genesis weapons roll a fresh innate and grade
  • If any copy you used was bound, the new piece is bound too

Changed

Two small things that came along with it.

🌊

The Abyssal Forge

  • Nerissa the Tidewright’s Abyssal reforging is now called the Abyssal Forge, so it is never confused with Reforging. It works exactly as before
🌐

Translations

  • The new text is translated into every supported language
