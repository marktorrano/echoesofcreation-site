Waypoints & Maps

An Echoes of Creation guide

On this page

  2. Map Progression

Waypoints

Each map has one waypoint. Walk near it to discover it. Once discovered, you can teleport to it from any other waypoint.

Press E on a waypoint to open the teleport panel.

Map Progression

Village - Safe zone (NPCs, shops)

Forest Path - Lv 1-5

Dark Woods - Lv 4-7

Goblin Hills - Lv 8-13

Cursed Caverns - Lv 14-20

Ancient Ruins - Lv 21-27

Creator's Gate - Lv 28-30 (final boss)

Portals

Walk into portals at map edges to travel between adjacent zones. Portals show the destination name and level range.

Minimap

The minimap is in the top-right corner. It shows:

  • Blue dot: your position
  • Red dots: monsters
  • Green dots: NPCs
  • Cyan diamond: waypoint
  • Yellow dots: chests
  • Gold arrow: quest objective direction
