Global Rankings

Press Y or click the Leaderboard button (top-right) to view global player rankings.

The board refreshes once an hour, on the hour (UTC). Your latest stats are submitted in the background whenever your game saves — but the rankings themselves don't move until the next hourly evaluation. The footer shows when the current snapshot was taken and when the next refresh is due.

Categories

Three ranking tabs:

  • Power — Combat Power (overall character strength roll-up)
  • Level — highest character level
  • Guilds — guilds ranked against each other, not players

Click a tab to switch between rankings.

Guild Rankings

The Guilds tab ranks guilds by Guild Level, which a guild raises by completing guild orders. Ties are broken by total lifetime contribution, then by which guild was founded first.

Each row shows the guild's rank, name, leader, member count and level. The top three get the same gold/silver/bronze medals as the player boards, and your own guild is highlighted in gold.

If your guild sits outside the ranks shown, it's pinned at the bottom of the list so you can always see where you stand.

Unlike the player boards this one is live — it reflects contribution the moment it lands, with no hourly snapshot to wait for.

Top-3 Medals

The top 3 of each category get a colored medal next to their name:

  • Gold — Rank 1
  • Silver — Rank 2
  • Bronze — Rank 3

Medals refresh with the snapshot each hour. Hold rank 1 at the top of an hour and you keep the gold dot until the next refresh.

Your Rank

Your entry is highlighted with a golden tint in the leaderboard.

The top 100 players are shown per category.

Your stats are submitted automatically whenever your game saves — within a couple of minutes of any score change, you're queued for the next snapshot.

Search & Player Cards

Use the search box under the category tabs to find ANY player by character name — including offline players, alternate characters, and the lowest levels (GM/dev accounts are excluded). Type at least 2 letters.

Click a player's row — here on the leaderboard OR on a member in your Guild roster — to open their card: class, level, faction, guild, CP, gender, and whether that character is currently Online (green) or Offline. From the card you can Invite to Party, Add Friend, or Chat.

Filters (Faction / Class)

The Filter button (top-right, above the table) opens a dropdown with two groups you can combine:

  • Faction — All / Light / Dark / Creation.
  • Class — All / Adventurer / Swordsman / Assassin / Ranger / Cleric.

Picks combine (e.g. Dark + Ranger), the button shows how many filters are active, and it all works together with the search box.

Each row also shows a small status dot beside the rank — green Active, blue Auto-farming, amber Away, grey Offline — so you can see who's online at a glance.

Combat Power (CP)

Combat Power is a single number summarising your overall character strength. It folds in: level, ATK, DEF, max HP/MP, accuracy, dodge, block chance, crit rate & damage, attack speed, talent points spent, gear upgrade levels, and filled gem sockets. Visible in your Stats panel header (C).

Whenever it moves, a banner counts your new total up on screen with the change beside it - green for a gain, red for a loss - so you can see straight away whether an upgrade, a socket or a talent point was worth it.

Upgrading gear, sockets, or talents will visibly move your CP — use it to compare loadouts.
