Loadouts

An Echoes of Creation guide

What a Loadout Saves

A Loadout is a saved build you can swap to in one click.

It stores all 15 gear slots, your auto-combat rotation and auto-heal slots, your hotbar, and which pet you have summoned.

It can also store your talent build, but that part is optional — see "Loadouts and Talents".

Open Loadouts from the side menu. Once you've saved one, a numbered quick-swap row appears near your shortcut buttons.

Where Your Other Gear Lives

A Loadout stores a LIST, not the items themselves.

Gear from a Loadout you aren't currently wearing sits in your bag, taking up slots and weight as usual.

Applying a Loadout just unequips what doesn't match and re-equips what does.

If your bag can't hold the gear coming off, the swap is refused rather than half-applied — free up space and try again.

If a piece was sold, traded or stored since you saved, that slot is left empty and the panel tells you which pieces it couldn't find.

When You Can Swap

Swap freely, even mid-fight — a Loadout for the class you're already playing applies in combat just like equipping a piece from your bag does.

The one exception is a Loadout tagged with a DIFFERENT class: changing class is blocked while you're in combat, and that block lifts a few seconds after your last hit, the same way teleporting and logging out do.

Those cross-class quick-swap buttons grey out while you're in combat so you can see the block before you click.

Loadouts and Talents

Saving a talent build into a Loadout costs 1 Respec Scroll — tick "Also save my talent build" when you save.

After that, switching between builds you've already saved is FREE, as often as you like.

Leave the tick off and the Loadout saves gear, rotation and hotbar only, at no cost.

Clearing a Loadout loses the talent build stored in it; saving one again costs another scroll.

Talent points earned since you saved stay unspent after a swap — nothing is lost.

Extra Loadout Slots

Every character starts with 2 Loadout slots.

Buy Loadout Slot from the Lumina Shop's Convenience tab to add more, one at a time, up to 6 total.

Loadout slots are per-character, like Bag Expansions.
