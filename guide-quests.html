Main Quests

6 main story quests from Elder Maren. They form a linear chain guiding you through all zones, from Forest Path to Creator's Gate.

Side Quests

12 side quests (2 per zone) from Mira and Gareth. These are kill or collect objectives that give bonus EXP, gold, and items.

Quest Indicators

Gold ! above NPC = new quest available

Gold ? = quest ready to turn in

Gray ? = quest in progress

No indicator = no quests for you right now

The dot on the Quests button (shortcut bar) only lights when a quest is genuinely ready to accept or turn in. Bounties on cooldown or past the 10-per-day limit don't count — they show greyed out in the quest log's Available tab with their cooldown or 'Daily limit' noted.

Quest Tracker

Active quests show on the right side of the screen with progress bars.

Incomplete objectives show which zone to travel to if you're on the wrong map.

Completed quests show the turn-in NPC name.

Press J to open the full quest log with details and rewards.

Click a quest name in the tracker to auto-navigate:

  • Wrong zone: your character walks through portals to reach the target zone, then Hunt mode activates.
  • Already in the right zone: Hunt mode activates automatically.
  • Objectives done: walks through portals to Village, then moves to the turn-in NPC.
  • Moving with WASD or toggling auto-combat off (Q) cancels quest auto-pathing.

Quest Pathing

A golden arrow guides you toward your active quest objective.

On the minimap: a pulsing gold arrow and diamond marker point to your destination.

On-screen: an arrow appears at the screen edge when your target is off-screen.

If the objective is on another map, the arrow points to the portal you need to take.

When objectives are complete, the arrow guides you back to the turn-in NPC.

Main quests take priority over side quests for pathing.

Hunt mode prioritizes quest target monsters. If a kill quest needs specific monsters (e.g. Goblin Scouts), auto-hunt will prefer those over other nearby enemies.

Collection Quests

Collect quests auto-count items already in your inventory when you accept them. You don't need to re-collect items you already have!

Story Cutscenes

Completing main quests triggers story cutscenes that reveal the lore of the Creator, the Light, and the Dark. Entering new zones for the first time also shows an atmospheric introduction. Cutscenes play once per save file.
