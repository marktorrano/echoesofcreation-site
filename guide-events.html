Events Panel

Open the Events panel from the Menu (☰ → Events) to see the special and limited-time events happening in the world.

Each event shows its name, schedule, and a short description. Tap an event for full details.

Sanctum Rush Hour

For one hour, the Awakened Forgotten One in the Forgotten Sanctum respawns 5 minutes after each defeat instead of the usual hour. Rally a party and farm the raid boss for keys, gear, and EXP while it lasts.
