Saving & Characters

An Echoes of Creation guide

Multiple Characters

You start with 5 character slots and can unlock up to 10 (2,000 Lumina per extra slot). Each character has independent stats, inventory, quests, and progress. Create and manage characters from the title screen.

Title Screen

The title screen lists your character slots (scroll if you have many). Occupied slots show name, level, class, and current map. Click Play to load, Create New for empty slots, or Delete to remove a character. Use 'Add Character Slot' to unlock more slots with Lumina, up to 10. Drag the ↕ handle to reorder.

Return to Title

Open the ESC menu and click 'Return to Title' to save your progress and switch characters. You can also quit the game from the title screen.

Auto-Save

Your game auto-saves every 60 seconds, on level up, when changing maps, and when exiting. You never need to manually save.

What's Saved

Everything: stats, level, EXP, equipment, inventory, upgrades, skills, quests, waypoints, storage, monsterpedia, currency, and current map location.

Cloud Saves

When logged in, your saves sync to the cloud automatically. You can access your characters from any device. If you play offline, saves remain local until you log in again.
