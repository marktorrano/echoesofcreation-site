Major Content Update

The Sundering

The world's spine has cracked. A new continent rises from the wound.

Target release: August 17, 2026 — dates may shift as we polish

The death of The Forgotten One did more than crack a seal — it broke the world itself. Across the new land bridge lies the Sundering: a torn continent where three powers pull the pieces their way, and something older stirs beneath the Scar. This is the largest update Echoes of Creation has ever received.

A New Continent

Seven new zones spanning levels 31–50, from faction capitals to the endgame crater.

🏰

Three Faction Capitals

  • Dawnspire Bastion — seat of the Light
  • Umbral Hold — refuge of the Dark
  • The Wellspring — sanctuary of Creation
  • Living cities: roaming citizens, guards, merchants, and your new home waypoint
⛰

Field Zones

  • Reclaimed Dominion Lv 31–39 — Light territory
  • The Umbral Reach Lv 31–39 — Dark badlands under an ember dusk
  • Nascent Wilds Lv 31–39 — Creation's overgrown frontier
  • The Sundering Scar Lv 42–50 — the neutral endgame wound, where a Lv100 titan sleeps in the crater

Choose Your Faction

Light, Dark, or Creation — the choice shapes where you fight, where you respawn, and who stands beside you.

⚔

Faction Life

  • Pledge to one of three factions and gain your capital as home ground
  • Faction members respawn at their own HQ
  • Each faction holds its own field zone with its own monsters, bosses, and loot
  • The Scar belongs to no one — all three factions send expeditions
👑

New Bosses

  • Nine faction bosses — three champions for each of Light, Dark, and Creation — plus the guardians of the neutral Scar
  • Light: Solarch the Fallen Dawn, the shielded Dawnwarden, and the Oracle of the First Light with her orbiting halo of ammunition
  • Dark: the greatsword tyrant Umbral Dreadlord; Nightmaw, the Long Hunger — an eyeless horror that rains bolts from the sky and ignites the arena from the edge inward, the only safe ground at his feet; and Dreadplate, the Unbroken — an armoured avalanche that leaps onto your position and raises an impenetrable guard
  • Creation: the Thorn Titan and two guardians yet to be unearthed
  • New boss mechanics throughout: inverted ring zones you dodge inward, protective wards that turn an ally into a hazard, and Trembling — get touched by the wrong boss and his next wail sends you running in blind panic

New Gear & Collections

Three new armor lines, a reworked collection book, and long-haul goals worth months of hunting.

🛡

Sundering Armor

  • Dawnweave, Duskweave, and Rootweave sets — one per faction
  • Ultra-rare Exalted versions of every piece
  • Drops from faction bosses and field monsters — and yes, they are tradeable
  • New upgrade materials: Elorite and Sunderite, crystallised from the break itself
📖

Collections Reworked

  • Every Sundering monster drops relics and cores feeding a two-line collection book
  • Second lines combine trophies across monsters — the real chase
  • New collection stats: PvE/PvP Accuracy and Dodge, Damage Reduction, Skill Damage, Critical Damage, carry Weight and more
  • Deposit straight from your bag with a tap-in number pad
  • Search, stat filters, and an "Available" toggle to find where your loot goes
  • Gear collections extended to the Genesis tier

A World Reborn

The old world grew to meet the new one.

🏗

Bigger, Denser, Alive

  • Characters, monsters, and architecture rescaled — the world has real presence now
  • Faction capitals built as living cities: plazas, arches, altars, roaming citizens, birds overhead
  • Hunting fields roughly doubled in monster density across the continent
  • Every Sundering species fights back with a full kit — spinning AoEs, leaping colossi, exploding cores
💎

Loot Worth Hunting

  • Every species drops its own relic and core — 34 new collectibles feeding the new book
  • Boss armor drops roll straight from the server with visible bestiary rates
  • Sundering armor is tradeable — hunt it, wear it, or sell it on the marketplace
  • Zone capstone collections for the long-haul: months of goals for completionists

Combat & Quality of Life

⚡

Combat Changes

  • Accuracy overhaul: high accuracy now lands reliably against monsters — no more phantom misses in PvE
  • New PvP hit model: match your rival's Dodge with Accuracy and your strikes are guaranteed
  • New status effects with on-screen indicators: Trembling, Fear, and a visible timer for Stun
  • Over a dozen new monster species across the continent, each with its own collection
🔧

Quality of Life

  • Chat loot filters — choose which pickup messages appear (gold, materials, equipment, Lumina, pets, gems)
  • Bestiary upgrades: bigger pages, armor set entries, cleaner drop tables
  • Collection and bestiary panels enlarged for easier browsing
  • Storage expanded to 250 slots

Numbers, names, and dates reflect the current test build and may change before release.
