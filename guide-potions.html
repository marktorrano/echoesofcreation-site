Village Portal Scroll: Teleport to village (5g)

Potion Cooldown

After drinking a potion, that type goes on a 4-second cooldown before it can be used again.

Cooldowns are per category and independent: HP, MP, and each buff stat each cool down separately, so a fresh HP drink never blocks your MP potion.

The same cooldown applies whether you drink manually (hotbar key/click) or it fires automatically, so you can't chug past the limit.

A darkened radial sweep plus a countdown appears over any potion on your hotbar and over the HP/MP auto-use slots while it's cooling down.

Special items (portal scrolls, charms, keys, expansions) are not affected.

Active Effects Panel

Your active buffs appear as a row of icons with countdowns at the bottom-left of the screen.

Click any of those icons to open the Active Effects panel — a closeable, scrollable list of every effect currently on you, each with its icon, name (and the potion or skill it came from), and remaining time.

A potion buff and a skill buff on the same stat are listed separately. Auto-Loot and Scroll of Protection charges show here too.

Close it with the X, by clicking outside it, or with Esc.
