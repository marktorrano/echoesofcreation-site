Movement

W / A / S / D to move.

Click on the ground to walk to that location.

Your character follows the mouse path.

Attack

F or Z to attack the nearest monster.

Click on a monster to target it, then attack.

TAB cycles through nearby targets.

SPACE to jump.

Interact

E picks up the nearest item on the ground, and interacts with nearby NPCs, waypoints and chests.

Loot comes first: if a dropped item is in reach, E takes it — one item per press, so press again for the next one.

You can also click loot, NPCs and chests directly.

Hotbar

Keys 1-9 activate the main hotbar slots.

There's also an extra top row (no keys) — click those slots to use them. Toggle it with the chevron next to the bar.

Drag skills or items onto slots from the Skill (K) or Inventory (I) screens.

Drag a skill out of a slot and drop it anywhere to unassign it (or right-click to clear).

Right-click a slot to toggle auto-cast on/off.

U - Collection

M - World Map

Wardrobe - Menu (☰) → Wardrobe

[ / ] - Rotate Camera

F1 - This Help Menu

` (Backtick) - Toggle Power Saving Mode (or the moon button beside Auto Hunt)

Changelog - Open from the Menu (☰)

Mail - Click the envelope icon in the icon bar (top-right)

ESC - Untarget / Pause Menu

Every icon in the top-right bar can be moved or removed — see "Customize Your Icon Bar" below.

Customize Your Icon Bar

The two rows of icons in the top-right corner are yours to arrange.

Open it from the Menu (☰) → Customize HUD.

Drag an icon into either row to put it on your HUD, or drag it down to the tray to take it off. Drop an icon between two others to change the order. If you'd rather not drag, double-click an icon and it moves for you.

Each row holds up to 12 icons. Press Save to apply, Cancel to leave things as they were, or Reset to Default to put the bar back the way it shipped.

The Menu (☰) icon is fixed in place and can't be removed — it's how you get back to this screen.

Your arrangement is saved on this device, so it stays put between sessions and doesn't affect your other characters.

The arrow next to the bar still hides every icon except the Menu when you want a clear view.

Chat Commands

Type these in the chat box:

ENTER - Open chat / send message

/w <name> <message> - Message a player privately (also /whisper, /msg)

/r <message> - Reply to the last person who messaged you (also /reply)

Each private conversation opens as its own tab in the chat box; click the ✕ on the tab to close it. See Multiplayer → Direct Messages.

Translate Chat

Tap the 🌐 button on any chat message to translate it into your language — the translation appears under the original (which stays). Tap 🌐 again to hide or re-show it.

Your language defaults to your device's; change it in Options → Gameplay → "Chat Translate To".

Note: translating a message sends its text to Google Translate to be translated.

Customizing Key Bindings

Almost every key above can be remapped. Open Options (O or the side menu ☰) and select the Controls tab.

Click a binding, then press the new key — Esc cancels. If the key is already used by another binding, you'll be asked to confirm; accepting swaps the two so nothing is left unbound.

Reset to Defaults restores the original layout. Bindings are saved on your device and shared across all characters.

Note: Esc (menu/cancel) and the camera-rotate keys ([ and ]) are fixed and can't be remapped.

Camera

Mouse scroll wheel to zoom in/out.

[ and ] to rotate the camera left/right.

Pinch-to-zoom on mobile/touchscreen.

Mobile Controls

On mobile devices, touch controls appear automatically:

  • Virtual Joystick (bottom-left): Drag to move your character.
  • Attack Button (bottom-right): Tap or hold to attack. Hold for continuous attacks.
  • Skill Buttons: 4 buttons around the attack button mapped to hotbar slots 1-4. Shows skill/item icons, cooldowns, and item quantities.
  • Tap an empty skill button or long-press any skill button to open the assignment picker. Choose from your learned skills or consumable items.
  • Tap the ground to walk to that location (same as click-to-move on desktop).
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in/out.

Ride / Dismount

The Ride button on your shortcut bar calls your mount, and pressing it again sets you down.

It is hidden until you own a mount. Like every other icon on that bar you can drag it somewhere else, or off it entirely, from Customize Interface.
