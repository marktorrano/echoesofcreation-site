Friends List

Open the Friends panel with the L key, or from the Menu (☰ → Friends).

You'll see all your friends, who is online, and where they are right now.

The coloured dot shows their activity:

  • Green "Active" — at the keyboard, playing right now.
  • Blue "Auto-farming" — auto-hunt is running hands-off (no recent input).
  • Amber "Away" — idle or in Power Saving mode (auto-fighting but not at the keyboard).
  • Grey "Offline" — not currently online.

This lets you tell who's really around versus just AFK-farming. Each dot shows level, class, and current zone too.

Adding Friends

Type a player's character name into the "Add friend by character name" box, then press Add (or Enter).

They receive a friend request, and once they accept you become friends.

If that player had already sent YOU a request, adding them back accepts it instantly. You can have up to 200 friends.

Requests

Incoming friend requests appear at the top of the Friends panel with Accept and Decline buttons.

A red dot on the Friends entry in the Menu (☰) means you have requests waiting.

Requests you have sent show under "Pending" until the other player responds — press Cancel on a pending request to withdraw it.

Online Status & Location

A friend counts as online while they are actively playing; their status and location refresh about every 30 seconds.

Location is the zone they are currently in, such as Eloria Village or Forest Path.

Use the Whisper button on a friend's row to start a private conversation with them (see Multiplayer → Private Messages).

Use the Invite button to invite an online friend to your party from anywhere - they don't need to be on your map. The button only appears for online friends, and greys out if your party is full or you aren't the leader.

Remove a friend at any time with the ✕ button on their row.
